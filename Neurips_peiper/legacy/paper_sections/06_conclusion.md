# 6. Conclusion

Mapping semantics to discrete, rule-governed logical coordinates offers a definitive solution to categorical hallucinations by design. While this framework introduces challenges, such as the initial human cost of designing local ontologies and the potential bottleneck of the parsing step, it provides a rigorous and explainable alternative to the "black box" of continuous embeddings. By embracing restricted logical boundaries and speaking strictly of what can be represented within a grammar of sense, we can achieve true neurosymbolic verification. The future of reliable AI lies not in the endless expansion of statistical models, but in the structural rigor of the spaces in which they operate. By adopting a world-local, Tractarian approach, we move toward systems that do not merely approximate truth but formally guarantee the sense of their assertions.

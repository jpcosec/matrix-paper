# Reference: Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus

Wittgenstein, L. (1922). *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus*. 

Key propositions cited:
- 1.1: The world is the totality of facts, not of things.
- 2.013: Every thing is, as it were, in a space of possible atomic facts. This space I can imagine empty, but I cannot imagine the thing without the space.
- 3.032: To present in language anything which 'contradicts logic' is as impossible as in geometry to present by its coordinates a figure which contradicts the laws of space.

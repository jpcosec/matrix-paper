# 6. Limitations and Conclusion

## 6.1 Limitations and Research Agenda
- **Parser Bottlenecks:** The NL2Projection step relies on LLMs, which means the compilation step itself can fail if the LLM misinterprets the text. Research is needed into self-correcting parsers.
- **Design Cost:** Defining local worlds and applicability rules ($\Gamma$) requires human expert engineering. However, LLMs can be used to help bootstrap these structures.
- **Rigidity:** Strict boolean logic struggles with poetic or highly metaphorical language where boundaries are intentionally blurred.

## 6.2 Conclusion
Mapping semantics to discrete, rule-governed logical coordinates guarantees zero categorical hallucinations by design. By adopting a world-local, Tractarian approach, we move beyond the "black box" of continuous embeddings toward systems that formally guarantee the sense of their assertions. The AI community must embrace restricted logical boundaries—speaking strictly "of what can be said"—to achieve true neurosymbolic verification.

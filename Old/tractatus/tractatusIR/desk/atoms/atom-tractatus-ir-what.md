# Tractatus IR — What it is

## Answer

A computational implementation of Wittgenstein's Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus ontology for semantic disambiguation. Concepts (objects) are represented as stable identifiers that project into contextual logical spaces (contexts K) as typed binary signatures, enabling discriminative representation, semantic hashing, index-based lookup, and horizontal (similarity/contrast) relation computation. The system distinguishes three representation dimensions: vertical/hierarchical (is-a chains), local/discriminative (contextual feature bits), and horizontal/relational (similarity/contrast scores).

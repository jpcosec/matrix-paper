# Tractatus IR — Why

## Answer

Provides the semantic bedrock for the hum-ecosystem's concept disambiguation, knowledge representation, and logical inference machinery. Downstream tools (hum scrapper, runtime dispatchers, knowledge graph builders) depend on tractatusIR to resolve ambiguous concept references into well-formed propositional facts (Sachverhalte) with defined sense boundaries (sinnvoll/sinnlos/unsinnig).

# Tractatus IR — Purpose

## Answer

To provide a formally-grounded, computationally-executable model of semantic representation based on Wittgenstein's picture theory of language, where meaning is derived from the structured connection between names and objects within bounded logical spaces, and where every bit in a representation is interpretable (named, not opaque).

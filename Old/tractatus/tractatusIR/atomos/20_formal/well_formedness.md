## Core Claim

Well-formedness is context-sensitive typed admissibility.

The source corpus includes at least:

- one-hot constraints
- multi-hot allowances
- boolean dimensions
- applicability conditions
- valid-mask mediated admissibility

## Early Encoding Rule

For exclusive dimensions, exactly one active bit is required.

For non-exclusive dimensions, zero, one, or many active bits may be allowed.

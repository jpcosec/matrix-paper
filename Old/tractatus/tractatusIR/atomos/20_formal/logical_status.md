Canonical status family:

- sinnvoll
- sinnlos
- unsinnig
- outside context

## Canonical Meaning

- `sinnvoll`: valid and informative in context
- `sinnlos`: valid but tautological or contradictory in denotation
- `unsinnig`: ill-formed or not projectable
- `outside_context`: structurally valid but excluded by active sense mask

This file defines the status family itself.
The tractarian relation between projection and the limit of sense lives in `atomos/05_tractatus/projection_and_limits_of_sense.md`.

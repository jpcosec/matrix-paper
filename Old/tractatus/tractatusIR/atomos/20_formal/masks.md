Canonical mask family:

- valid mask
- sense mask
- discriminative mask
- observed mask

## Canonical Role

Masks express at least four different restrictions:

- what applies ontologically
- what has sense in the current context or task
- what is currently useful for discrimination
- what is actually observed or measured

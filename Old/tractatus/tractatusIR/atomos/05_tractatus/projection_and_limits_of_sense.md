## Projection

Projection maps a sign or symbol under context into an interpretable signature or matrix position.

## Limits Of Sense

The source corpus preserves three principal statuses:

- `sinnvoll`
- `sinnlos`
- `unsinnig`

Later operational sources also add `outside_context` when a form is structurally valid but excluded by the active sense regime.

Detailed status meanings live in `atomos/20_formal/logical_status.md`.

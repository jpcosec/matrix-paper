## Core Distinction

- sign: perceptible vehicle such as a word, bit pattern, or surface label
- symbol: sign plus logical-syntactic use in context
- name: role of reference inside proposition structure

## Canonical Rule

The same sign can belong to different symbols depending on its use.

This is central to the source corpus and must remain explicit in the canonical model.

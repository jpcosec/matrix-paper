## Canonical Role

Logical form is the enabling grammar that determines what combinations count as admissible representation.

Logical space is the space of possibilities opened by a context.

In the source corpus, dimensions function as local coordinate axes of that logical space.

Grammar `Γ` and context `K` jointly determine admissibility.

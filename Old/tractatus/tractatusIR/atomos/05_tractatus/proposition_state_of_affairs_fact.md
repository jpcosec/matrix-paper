## Canonical Distinction

- proposition: projection of a possible situation
- state of affairs: possible combination such as `D(c) = v` or `R(a,b)`
- fact: asserted or evidenced state of affairs

## Operational Reading In Source Corpus

Later sources model facts as typed propositions and relations as well-formed applications over concepts and values.

Rules of logical form are not themselves ordinary facts.

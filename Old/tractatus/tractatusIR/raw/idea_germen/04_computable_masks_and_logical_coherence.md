# Computable Masks And Logical Coherence

## Why Masks Appear

Once the representational system is read as a logical notation, another problem becomes unavoidable.

It is not enough to know that a bit exists in the dictionary.
We must also know:

- whether it applies to the current concept
- whether it has sense in the current context
- whether it is currently informative for discrimination
- whether it has actually been observed or measured

This is where computable masks enter.

## Sinn In This Notation

At the tractarian level, `Sinn` is the possible situation presented by a proposition before truth is decided.

In this notation, the closest computational equivalent is not the raw bit vector by itself.
It is the admissible projected region left open after contextual restrictions are applied.

So:

```text
Sinn = projected possibility under active masks
```

This means masks do not merely filter data after the fact.
They define the space within which projected articulations count as having sense at all.

## The Core Distinction

The system should not collapse all inactive or zero-valued bits into the same meaning.

For a given branch, a `0` may mean very different things:

- false
- unknown
- not applicable
- irrelevant under current context

So mask structure is needed to preserve logical coherence.

## Four Main Masks

The current formulation already points toward four main masks.

### 1. Valid mask

What ontologically or structurally applies to the concept.

Example:

- `estado_vital` applies to organisms
- `estado_vital` does not apply to `martillo`

This is the first boundary of possible sense.

### 2. Sense mask

What has sense in the current context or task.

Example:

- in `distinguir verduras`, `precio` may be turned off
- in another context, `precio` may be active and meaningful

This mask is the most direct computational approximation of local `Sinn`.

### 3. Discriminative mask

What is actually useful now for separating the current candidates.

Example:

- `planta` may be true of all current candidates and thus useless discriminatively
- `parte_comestible` may be the active separating question

### 4. Observed mask

What has actually been measured, confirmed, or supplied as evidence.

This matters when a value is not merely false but missing.

## From Facts To Sense-Space

Our factual layer is composed of typed projected articulations such as:

- `D_PARTE_COMESTIBLE(C_LECHUGA) = hoja`
- `D_COLOR(C_AUTO) = rojo`
- `imports(MODULE_API, LIB_FASTAPI)`

But these atomic facts do not automatically belong to the same active logical space in every context.

Masks determine:

- which facts can obtain here as admissible articulations
- which dimensions are currently meaningful
- which deeper branches are open
- which regions of the tensorial space count as part of the present local world

So masks do not merely clean up facts.
They define the active local factual space.

In that sense:

```text
local welt = factual articulations admitted by the current masked space
```

## Masks As Computable Restriction

These masks operationalize the logical boundary of the system.

They determine not just storage, but what expressions are admissible, meaningful, and informative.

So masks are the computational mechanism that restricts coherence.

They are also what turn a raw tensorial or matricial space into a local space of sense rather than a flat array of possible coordinates.

## From Tractarian Status To Computable Status

The tractarian triplet becomes operational here.

### Sinnvoll

An expression is meaningful when:

- it is well formed
- it is applicable
- it lies within the active local sense regime
- it selects a proper non-empty subset of the local universe

### Sinnlos

An expression may be well formed yet non-informative.

This includes local tautological or vacuous forms.

Examples:

- a statement that accepts every candidate in the local context
- a contradiction expressible inside the notation but yielding no real local discrimination

### Unsinnig

An expression is nonsensical when it cannot even be projected under the local grammar.

Examples:

- value outside the allowed set for a dimension
- dimension applied where it does not apply
- mixed incompatible dimensions in one ill-formed structure

We can now restate the triplet more sharply:

- `sinnvoll`: valid projected articulation inside the active masked space of sense
- `sinnlos`: formally valid articulation that does not determine a proper possible region
- `unsinnig`: failed projection or failed admissibility

## Why This Is More Than Validation

Masks are not just implementation details for cleaner code.
They are the mechanism by which the system separates:

- possible but unobserved
- possible but irrelevant
- impossible here
- structurally invalid
- informative and admissible

Without them, the system drifts back toward flat bit manipulation and loses the logical discipline introduced by the tractarian layer.

Without them, we also lose the distinction between:

- factual articulation
- admissible sense
- contextual irrelevance
- non-applicability

## Masks And Branch-Local Structure

Masks also become crucial once context is treated as a discriminative tree.

If a concept falls under the `raiz` branch, then dimensions deeper inside the `hoja` branch are not merely false.
They are branch-inapplicable.

So the mask structure is what lets sparse branch-local dimensions remain coherent without pretending every inactive deep bit is just `0` in the same sense.

This is one of the places where the system becomes computationally serious.

It is also the place where local `welts` become definable, because branch-local activation decides which projected facts belong to the currently open local space.

## Practical Consequence

At the end of this stage, the system can be summarized as follows.

1. the discrimination game gives the representational heuristic
2. context and matrices give the local projection and expansion mechanism
3. the tractarian layer gives a logical reading of projection and admissibility
4. masks make those admissibility boundaries computable

This is the point where the notation stops being merely suggestive and starts behaving like a constrained internal logic.
